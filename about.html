About Me

My name is Robin Lim, age 53 years old, born in Kedah. I used to study Hotel Management and used to works as assising contractor. After that, I decided to start my own business in agriculture and now I am the owner of Superfruits Valley, a farm that specializes in growing fresh fruits in Chuping, Perlis . I have been running this business for 10 years and I am passionate about providing high-quality fruits to my customers. I alsu cultivate a variety of crops including vegetables and local fruits. My goal is to provide fresh, high-quality produce to the community,and I am commited to sustainable farming practces to ensure the health of the land and the quality of the fruits I grow.
